# Calc store listing draft

## App name

Calc

## Short description

Calculate, save records, and track debts, invoices and payments.

## Full description

Calc brings everyday calculations and saved records together in one place.

Save calculations with names and notes, review debts and invoices, and record payments as they are settled. Search your records and use calendar filters to find the dates you need.

Features:

- Calculator with editable calculations and saved results
- Saved records with names, notes and edit history
- Debt and invoice lists with totals and payment details
- Search and date filters
- Local saving on this device, including offline use
- Multiple interface languages and optional button sounds

Changes are saved on your device. Cloud transfer and sync are currently unavailable; saved records do not automatically move to another device.
