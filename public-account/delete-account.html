CALC · ACCOUNT SUPPORT

Delete your Calc account

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app. Deleting your Calc account does not delete your Google account.

Request deletion by email

Send your request from the email address used for your Calc account to laertkarap@gmail.com, with the subject Delete my Calc account.

Email a deletion request

This opens your email app. The request is sent only when you send the email. If you cannot use the button, copy the address and subject into your email service.

We verify account ownership before removing data and reply with the next steps and expected completion time. If you no longer have access to your account email, explain that in your message so we can discuss verification.

Never send your password, sign-in code or recovery code.

What deletion removes

  • Your Calc sign-in account and its authentication profile.
  • Account-linked cloud calculations, debts, invoices, payments, titles, notes and comments.
  • Edit history and records previously deleted using the app's recoverable-delete feature.

Account deletion is permanent. Export anything you want to keep before requesting it.

Copies and information that remain

Files already exported to Google Drive or shared with other apps are separate copies. Delete those in their respective services. Guest records and copies on other offline devices are not remotely erased; clear Calc's app data or uninstall it on those devices. Manage any Android system backups in your device's backup settings.

A minimal deletion marker containing the old Calc account ID and request timestamp is retained indefinitely for security: it prevents old devices or still-valid sessions from uploading deleted records again. It contains no email, calculations, amounts or notes. Support correspondence and service-provider security logs are separate from app records; ask about these when requesting deletion.

Delete from the app

In versions with account deletion, sign in, open Account → Delete account, read the confirmation and verify your identity with your password or Google. This also removes that account's records and queued changes on the device where you perform deletion. If the option is missing or deletion cannot finish, use the email request above.
